Get clear on the kind of tint you need

The phrase residential window tinting Alpharetta can point to film for a home, but Metropolitan Tinting’s service is for vehicle windows. We don’t list house-window installation. If your car needs tint and you’d like the appointment at your residence, we can come to your home or place of business. You can also arrange an appointment at our Alpharetta facility.

This distinction is worth sorting out before you call. A car appointment at your home is still automotive tinting, not a project for the windows in your house. If you’re comparing providers, check that the work they offer matches the glass you want treated.

Why choose Metropolitan Tinting

We install LLumar Window Film, which our business identifies as a high-quality film. The right choice for your vehicle depends on what you want the tint to do. Drivers often ask about reducing glare, blocking harmful UV rays, helping keep the cabin cooler, adding privacy, and limiting interior fading. Tint can also change the appearance of a car.

It helps to explain your priorities rather than assume every film or installation is the same. We can discuss the vehicle service you’re looking for and arrange a mobile appointment or a visit to the Alpharetta facility.

What to prepare before you book

A short checklist can make the first conversation more useful:

  • Confirm that you want tint on a car, not on the windows of your house.
  • Have the vehicle available for the appointment.
  • Decide where you’d prefer the work done, at home, at your workplace, or at the Alpharetta facility.
  • Think about your main concern, such as glare, sun exposure, privacy, cabin comfort, or fading.
  • Ask about the film and the appointment details before setting a time.

Avoid booking based only on a search phrase. It can lead to a mismatch between the type of glass you have and the service a company provides. If your goal is a home improvement project, ask a provider specifically about residential building windows. If the job is for your car, describe it as vehicle tinting when you contact us.
