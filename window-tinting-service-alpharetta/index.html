Know what the appointment covers

When you’re looking for a window tinting service Alpharetta, start by confirming that the work is for a car. Metropolitan Tinting installs custom tint on vehicle windows. We don’t offer tint for house windows, so it’s useful to be specific about the vehicle and the kind of appointment you need.

Car tint may help keep the interior cooler and more comfortable, block harmful UV rays, reduce glare, and help protect the interior from fading. It can also provide privacy and change the look of your vehicle. Which benefit matters most is a good starting point for a conversation before you book.

A simple checklist for booking

Have these details in mind when you contact us:

  • The main reason you’re considering tint, such as glare, privacy, UV exposure, comfort, or interior fading.
