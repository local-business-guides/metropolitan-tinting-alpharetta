What to expect from custom window tinting

Custom car window tinting involves installing film on your vehicle's windows. The service is intended for cars, and the film can offer several practical benefits: helping keep the vehicle cooler and more comfortable, reducing glare, blocking harmful UV rays, and helping reduce interior fading. Tint can also add privacy and alter the vehicle's appearance.

Before calling, note which of those outcomes you care about most. If your concern is glare, for example, say so rather than asking only for a certain look. If you're mainly thinking about privacy or interior fading, mention that too. You can ask the installer to discuss your needs and what the process covers for your car. Confirm whether you'll meet at your home, workplace, or the Alpharetta facility, and ask what preparation is needed for that appointment.
